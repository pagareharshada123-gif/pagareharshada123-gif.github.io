04 — FEATURED RESEARCH

My research project.

B.Sc. RESEARCH PROJECT

Isolation of Hydrocarbon-Degrading and Biosurfactant-Producing Bacterial Isolates

The research investigated bacterial isolates obtained from oil-contaminated environments and evaluated their hydrocarbon degradation and biosurfactant-producing capabilities.

The study predominantly identified isolates belonging to the Pseudomonas genus, demonstrating their potential as eco-friendly and cost-effective agents for the bioremediation of petroleum-contaminated soil and water.

Microbiology Bioremediation Hydrocarbon Degradation Biosurfactants
