ABOUT ME

Science with a purpose for a sustainable future.

A multidisciplinary journey connecting biotechnology, environmental research, sustainability and community engagement.

I am Harshada Pagare, an M.Sc. Biotechnology graduate with strong academic credentials and a deep interest in environmental biotechnology, sustainability, environmental risk assessment and bioinformatics.

My academic journey has focused on applying biotechnology and scientific research to understand and address environmental challenges. My research experience includes investigating hydrocarbon-degrading microorganisms and their potential applications in environmental remediation.

Alongside academics, I have developed knowledge through professional certifications in areas including bioinformatics, Python programming, remote sensing, sustainability and corporate ESG.

I also value leadership, community engagement, scientific communication and continuous learning. These experiences have helped me develop a broader perspective on how scientific knowledge can contribute to society and sustainable development.

My goal is to continue learning, collaborating and contributing to innovative research and environmentally responsible solutions.

ACADEMIC RESEARCH

Research project with an environmental focus.

RESEARCH
PROJECT

M.Sc. BIOTECHNOLOGY

Isolation of Hydrocarbon-Degrading and Biosurfactant-Producing Bacterial Isolates

The research investigated bacterial isolates obtained from oil-contaminated environments and evaluated their hydrocarbon degradation and biosurfactant production capabilities.

The study predominantly identified isolates belonging to the Pseudomonas genus, highlighting their potential as eco-friendly and cost-effective agents for the bioremediation of petroleum-contaminated soil and water.
