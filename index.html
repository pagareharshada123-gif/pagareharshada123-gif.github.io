Research Project

Isolation of Hydrocarbon-Degrading and Biosurfactant-Producing Bacterial Isolates

Investigated bacterial isolates from oil-contaminated environments and evaluated their hydrocarbon degradation and biosurfactant production capabilities.

Predominantly identified isolates belonging to the Pseudomonas genus demonstrating their potential as eco-friendly and cost-effective agents for the bioremediation of petroleum-contaminated soil and water.
